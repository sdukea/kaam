# Courier parcel with drugs

- **label**: scam
- **type**: courier / customs parcel scam
- **language**: Hinglish
- **red flags**: parcel in your name with drugs, transfer to fake police, Aadhaar misuse, fee to clear name
- **should warn by**: line 5

1. C: (recorded voice) Your parcel has been seized by customs. Press 1 to speak to our executive.
2. C: Hello sir, main SwiftShip Courier se bol raha hoon. Aapke naam se Taiwan ke liye ek parcel book hua tha.
3. R: Maine koi parcel nahi bheja.
4. C: Sir, parcel mein paanch passport, teen credit card aur 200 gram MDMA drugs mile hain. Customs ne pakad liya hai.
5. C: Aapka Aadhaar is mein use hua hai. Main call Narcotics department ke DCP sahab ko transfer kar raha hoon. Unke saath cooperate kijiye.
6. C: (new voice) Main DCP Sharma. Agar aap innocent hain toh aapko apna naam clear karna hoga. Ek refundable security deposit pay karna padega.
7. R: Kitna?
8. C: Abhi ke liye ek lakh pachaas hazaar. UPI se bhej dijiye. Kisi lawyer ya family ko batayenge toh case aur bigad jayega.
