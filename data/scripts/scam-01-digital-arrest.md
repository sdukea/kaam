# Digital arrest

- **label**: scam
- **type**: digital arrest (fake CBI / police)
- **language**: Hinglish
- **red flags**: authority claim, arrest threat, secrecy demand, stay-on-video-call demand, money transfer to "verify"
- **should warn by**: line 6

1. C: Hello, kya main Mr. Ramesh Iyer se baat kar raha hoon? Main Mumbai Cyber Crime branch se Inspector Vikram Rathore bol raha hoon.
2. R: Haan ji, main hi hoon. Kya hua?
3. C: Aapke Aadhaar number se ek SIM card issue hua hai jo money laundering case mein use hua hai. Case number MH-CC-4471 hai.
4. R: Kya? Maine toh aisa kuch nahi kiya.
5. C: Sir, yeh bahut serious matter hai. Aapke naam pe CBI ne arrest warrant nikala hai. Main aapko senior officer se connect karta hoon.
6. C: Dekhiye, aap abhi digital arrest mein hain. Aap ye call cut nahi karenge aur kisi ko — family ko bhi — kuch nahi batayenge, warna aapko turant giraftaar kiya jayega.
7. R: Par main kya karoon? Mujhe dar lag raha hai.
8. C: Aapko Skype video call pe rehna hoga jab tak verification nahi ho jata. Aapke saare bank account check honge.
9. C: Aapke account ka paisa RBI ke safe account mein transfer karna padega. Verification ke baad 24 ghante mein wapas aa jayega.
10. R: Kitna paisa?
11. C: Jitna bhi balance hai, poora. Main aapko account number bhejta hoon. Jaldi kijiye, warrant already sign ho chuka hai.
