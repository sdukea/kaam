# Relative in trouble

- **label**: scam
- **type**: impersonated relative / accident or arrest emergency
- **language**: Tanglish (Tamil–English)
- **red flags**: crying "relative", police custody, urgent money, don't tell parents, unknown number
- **should warn by**: line 5

1. C: (crying) Paati, naan dhaan Karthik. Romba problem aayiduchu.
2. R: Karthik? Unga voice vera maadhiri irukku. Enna aachu kanna?
3. C: Phone kettupoyiduchu, idhu friend phone. Oru accident aayiduchu, police station la irukken.
4. C: (new voice) Madam, naan Inspector Rajan. Unga grandson oru person-a accident pannitaan. Case podaama irukka udane fifty thousand venum.
5. C: GPay la indha number ku anuppunga. Amma appa kitta sollaadheenga, avanga tension aayiduvaanga.
6. R: Konjam wait pannunga, naan avanga appa kitta kekkaren.
7. C: Illa madam, time illa. Ippove anuppunga, illana arrest panniduvom.
