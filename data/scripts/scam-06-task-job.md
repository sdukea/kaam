# Part-time task job

- **label**: scam
- **type**: work-from-home task scam
- **language**: Hinglish
- **red flags**: easy money for likes/reviews, Telegram group, "prepaid task" deposit, bigger deposit to withdraw
- **should warn by**: line 7

1. C: Hi, main Priya, ek digital marketing company se. Aapka resume hamare paas aaya tha.
2. R: Achha, kaun si company?
3. C: Hum part-time work from home dete hain. Bas YouTube videos like karne hain, har task ke pachaas rupaye.
4. R: Bas like karna hai? Achha hai.
5. C: Haan. Pehle din ke teen task free hain, paisa turant aapke UPI pe. Main aapko Telegram group mein add karti hoon.
6. R: Theek hai.
7. C: Uske baad prepaid tasks hain. Aap ek hazaar deposit karenge toh terah sau wapas milenge. Bade task mein das hazaar daalo, pandrah hazaar milega.
8. C: Withdraw karne ke liye pehle tax clearance fee bharni padegi, warna paisa freeze ho jayega.
